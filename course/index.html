Health of Nations

You are the Minister of Health of a whole country: a fixed budget, a population with hidden preferences, six years and two elections. Every mechanic is a concept from this course: QALY league tables, value-of-information studies, copays and moral hazard, discrete choice experiments, equity-weighted funding, prevention as capital. The game's Codex links each concept back here.

For teachers

Using this in a paper

The chapters fit a 12-week 300- or 400-level health economics paper: one chapter per week, plus weeks for the game capstone and revision. Each chapter ends with a seeded classroom run of Health of Nations; because the seed fixes the country, a class can compare strategies on identical terrain and submit results with the game's copy-result button or class leaderboard. Each chapter supports a 20-minute in-class activity. Ask students to predict before they move a slider, then explain any surprise. The discussion prompts work as tutorial questions or short essays.
